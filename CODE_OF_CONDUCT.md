# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Credit data sources and prior work accurately.
- Avoid presenting synthetic or estimated results as verified facts.
- Respect privacy: never contribute real patient-identifiable data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private
information without consent, or deliberately misrepresenting the accuracy of
the system.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository owner privately. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
